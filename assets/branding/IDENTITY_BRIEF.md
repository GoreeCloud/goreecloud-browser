# GoreeCloud Browser Identity Brief

## Status

GoreeCloud Browser artwork remains **pending**. No current candidate is approved.

This brief governs the next design cycle after multiple rejected concepts.

## Primary objective

Create an original GoreeCloud Browser identity that is immediately recognizable, visually coherent, and clearly appropriate for a web browser without resembling an established competing browser or an unrelated app category.

## Recognition target

The mark should pass a three-second unlabeled test:

- it should look intentional rather than abstract;
- it should be memorable after one viewing;
- it should plausibly belong to a browser/navigation product without reading primarily as GPS, cloud storage, security, networking, camera, media, or another category;
- it should remain identifiable at 16–32 px without relying on tiny detail.

## Preferred semantic territory

Explore concepts associated with the browser as a **view into the web**, **pages/content**, **tabs/layers**, **addressing/reaching information**, or **open exploration**, while avoiding literal copies of common browser-brand motifs.

The concept may be symbolic rather than literal, but its internal logic must be obvious.

## Hard exclusions

Do not base the primary identity on:

- segmented circles or radial blades;
- globe grids;
- orbiting animals or flame forms;
- rings resembling Opera;
- map pins, GPS markers, or compass needles;
- generic clouds;
- generic browser windows or four-pane tiles;
- standalone letter G;
- interlocking ribbons/folds with unclear meaning;
- apertures/lenses that resemble Chrome;
- shields, locks, keys, or security badges;
- network nodes, routers, antennas, or signal waves;
- download arrows;
- magnifying glasses;
- generic home icons;
- direct copies or close derivatives of Chrome, Chromium, Firefox, Edge, Safari, Opera, Brave, Vivaldi, Arc, or other major browser identities.

## Structural requirements

The master mark should have:

- one dominant silhouette;
- no more than two major internal structural ideas;
- strong positive/negative-space balance;
- geometry that survives monochrome conversion;
- a clear optical center;
- no dependence on gradients or shadows for recognition;
- no hairline strokes or micro-detail required for identity;
- enough safe-area margin for Android adaptive masks and desktop launcher treatments.

## Glaze UI requirements

The identity must follow the latest approved Stable Glaze UI iconography contract.

Glaze materials, color, depth, and light may enhance the mark after geometry is validated. Identity geometry remains authoritative.

The icon must support:

- full-color master;
- monochrome source;
- dark-context presentation;
- light-context presentation;
- high-contrast treatment;
- reduced-transparency treatment;
- Display, Standard, Compact, and Micro optical sizes.

## GoreeCloud character

The mark should feel native to GoreeCloud through proportion, Glaze UI material behavior, typography pairing, color relationships, and system-level polish rather than through a forced cloud glyph or literal G monogram.

It should look capable of sitting beside Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, Everkeep, Search, Vault, and other GoreeCloud identities while remaining unmistakably its own application.

## Candidate process

Future concepts must follow this order:

1. black-and-white silhouette concept;
2. unlabeled recognizability review;
3. category-confusion review;
4. similarity review against major browser brands;
5. 16, 32, 64, 128, and 256 px optical review;
6. monochrome confirmation;
7. only then apply Glaze UI color/material treatment;
8. explicit user approval before declaring official artwork.

A candidate that fails steps 2–4 should be rejected before further polish.

## Previously rejected concepts

- Glaze Navigation Ribbon — rejected.
- Horizon Portal — rejected.
- Aurora Window — rejected.
- Borealis Lens — rejected for Chrome-like similarity.
- Tidefold — rejected for poor recognizability.
- Waymark — rejected for GPS/location-category confusion.

## Production rule

Until explicit approval is recorded, the Browser must remain **artwork pending** and must not ship any candidate as official GoreeCloud Browser identity.
